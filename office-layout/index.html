Your arrangement

Drag pieces to move them. A desk carries its chair with it, and you can drag a chair on its own. Click a piece to select it; the arrows nudge it 1″ (hold Shift for 6″). Measurements show the clear space from the selected piece to the nearest wall or furniture on each side.

Spacing rules of thumb
  • 36″ behind a desk to pull a chair out and sit
  • 44–48″ when someone needs to walk behind a seated person
  • 60″ between desks whose chairs sit back to back
  • 36″ minimum for the main walkway to the stairs and door

What the desks need to do

  • Stand and sit. Every option is an electric sit-stand desk.
  • Some storage. People need somewhere to keep a notebook, headset and chargers so the desktops stay clear.
  • At least 10 in the room. The upstairs room is 17′ 8″ × 23′ 3″ with a door alcove, a phone booth, a post and the stair entry to work around. Each desk below is checked against the same to-scale plan, with 3 ft of chair space behind every desk.

Why white

Every desk on this list comes in white, and the chairs are light to match. The main reason is the light: the upstairs room should feel bright, open and energetic, and white furniture reflects the window light instead of soaking it up. There's a lighter reason too. Engineering works downstairs at black desks in black chairs. With us upstairs in all white, the building gets a little friendly heaven-and-hell rivalry, and we know which floor we'd rather be on.

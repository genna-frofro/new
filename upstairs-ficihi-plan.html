Additional asks

A ball chair

One balance ball chair anyone can swap into for part of the day. People will enjoy it, and it encourages active sitting: small, constant movements keep the core working and ease the stiffness of sitting still. It's included in the budget below.

A walking pad

The BDRs are currently competing for the most SQLs each month, and the prize is a standing desk. Once everyone has a standing desk, that prize needs a successor. The team was polled on what else they would like upstairs, and the answer was unanimous: a walking pad to use under a standing desk.

The exact model is still being chosen, with a focus on options that are inexpensive and quiet enough for calls. Quiet under-desk walking pads typically run about $100–$300. Here is an example of the kind being considered. It is not included in the totals below yet.
